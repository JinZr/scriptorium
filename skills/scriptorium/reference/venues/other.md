# Other venues

Use `other` for any venue that is neither a machine-learning conference nor a Nature-family journal, and when the author does not know the family yet. There is no venue-specific rubric: the frozen severity definitions apply as written.

## What to ask

- The venue name, if one is chosen, for the `venue` field.
- What that venue's reviewers weigh most, in the author's words, for `severity_notes`.
- The stage, the claims to check first, earlier reviews, and what to ignore, as for any brief.

## Defaults

`venue_family: "other"` with the stage the author gives; leave the lists empty if the author says "defaults".
